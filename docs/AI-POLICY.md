# AI usage policy

cream has rules for AI usage:

- **You must fully understand all the code you submit.** If you can't explain what your change does and how it fits into the rest of cream without asking an AI, don't submit it. This matters even more here than in most projects, because cream handles people's logins.

- **Issues and discussions can use AI help, but a human must review and edit them.** AI tends to be wordy and adds noise that hides the main point. Do your own research and cut it down before you post.

- **No AI-generated media** (images, art, video, audio and so on). Text and code are the only AI-generated content allowed, under the other rules here.

- **A human must run and test the code.**
  - Build cream and run your change for real, with a throwaway `CREAM_HOME` (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#setting-up)).
  - If you fix a bug, reproduce it yourself first. Don't just have the AI write a test.
  - Show how you tested it in the PR, for example the commands you ran and their output, or a screenshot of the TUI.

## There are humans here

cream is maintained by people. Every issue, discussion and pull request is read and reviewed by a person. Sending low-effort, unchecked work is disrespectful, because it moves all the checking onto the maintainer.

AI can do good work, but today that depends on the person using it. Until that gets better, these rules are here to protect the people who maintain the project.

## AI is welcome here

cream itself is written with plenty of AI help, and AI tools are part of how it is maintained. **This policy is not anti-AI.** It exists because of unreviewed work, not because of the tools.
